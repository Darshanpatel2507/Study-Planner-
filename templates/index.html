Everything a College Student Needs

Designed specifically to keep your assignments, practicals, and study schedules organized without unnecessary complexity.

Smart Task Management

Create, edit, organize, and track your study tasks with subjects, due dates, estimated hours, and status toggles.

Task Highlights

Instantly spotlight urgent tasks: Overdue warnings, Due Today alerts, Starred favorites, and High Priority items.

Study Progress Tracking

Real-time statistical breakdown of total, pending, and completed tasks with animated progress calculations.

Smart Recommendations

DSA-powered Priority Queue (Min-Heap) that automatically calculates and suggests your next most crucial study task.

Priority-Based Sorting
